# I really want to pet my cat
https://thornrime.itch.io/i-really-want-to-pet-my-cat

## Introduction
You're enjoying a peaceful Sunday afternoon at home when suddenly, the doorbell rings. You open the door to find a mysterious traveling spell merchant standing before you. He hands you a spell for never-aching-back. Sounds great… right?

Unfortunately, you quickly realize it’s not a blessing—it’s a curse! From now on, you're allergic to cats—just when you really want to pet your furry friend, who’s peacefully sleeping.

Your task is to gather items and build a device long enough to pet your cat from a safe distance. Hurry, you only have a moment before it wakes up!

And to think… none of this would have happened if you had just listened to your mother and never accepted spells from strangers.
